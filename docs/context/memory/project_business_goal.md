Orbitae's launch business target: reach roughly **$10,000 MRR** from only a
**small fraction of developers paying a monthly subscription** — the plan is
extensive **free marketing on Twitter/X** (and similar), not paid acquisition.

Implication for product decisions: features must solve **widespread, keenly-felt
developer pain** with AI coding agents (trust/verification, reviewing AI output,
token cost, control) well enough that devs will both pay AND organically share it.
Every core decision should be researched against "does this rectify a real pain
millions of devs have?" See [[feedback_research_first]], [[project_orbitae_vision]].
